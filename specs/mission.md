# Mission

## Why
Make the classic Iris dataset (150 flowers, 3 species, 4 measurements) explorable in a browser, so learners can *see* how the species differ and why it's a textbook classification example.

## Audience
Students and analysts learning exploratory data analysis (EDA), plus instructors who need a ready-made teaching demo.

## Purpose
- Show feature distributions by species.
- Reveal which measurements separate species (petals do; sepals mostly don't).
- Let users filter, compare and inspect the raw data themselves.

## Success looks like
A single file anyone can open offline, with no install, that answers "how do the three species differ?" in under a minute.

## Out of scope
Servers, user accounts, other datasets.
